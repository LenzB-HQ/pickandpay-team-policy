Delete your account

P&P Team accounts are created by your employer, so deleting an account is requested by you and approved by your employer. It is not instant.

How to ask (in the app)

  1. Open the app, go to Account and tap Delete my account.
  2. Read what will be deleted and kept, type DELETE and tap Ask to delete my account.
  3. Your owner is told and approves or rejects the request. (An owner’s own request is approved by the app’s administrator.) Until it is approved nothing changes, you can keep working, and you can cancel the request.

The request cannot be completed while you are checked in or on a delivery trip: check out or return first. The last owner of the company cannot be deleted.

If your owner does not answer

Email mlabeeb707@gmail.com with your full name, your username, your store and the words “Delete my account”. We will ask the company’s owner or administrator to decide and tell you the result within 7 days.

What is deleted or replaced

  • Your login and all your signed-in sessions.
  • Your phone number, picture and username (the username is replaced by a random one).
  • Your name, which is replaced by “Former employee”.
  • Your phone’s notification (push) details and any unused invite or check-in codes.

What stays

  • The company’s work records: your shifts, deliveries, bills, delivery places and alerts. They stay without your name (shown as “Former employee”) for as long as the company uses the app, because the company needs them for payroll, safety and legal records, and so that other people’s totals stay correct.
  • Free-text reasons and notes written by or about you (for example a note on a late delivery, the reason for a manual check-in, or an owner’s note when deciding a request) and any copies of them in the audit records stay as work records. They are not cleaned.
  • Our hosting provider, Supabase, keeps platform logs (which can include sign-in details such as your username, IP address and time) and database backups for its own retention period and then they expire. We cannot delete them earlier: platform logs are kept for about 1 day, and on our current plan no project backups are kept (if we upgrade, scheduled backups are kept for up to 7 days).

A request that is approved but not carried out expires after 7 days; you can ask again.

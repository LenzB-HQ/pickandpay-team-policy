P&P Team

P&P Team is a work app for the staff of one company. Staff check in and out by scanning a store QR code, delivery staff record their trips, and managers and owners follow attendance, late returns and alerts.

The app is invite-only: your employer creates your account and gives you an invite ID. There is no public sign-up.

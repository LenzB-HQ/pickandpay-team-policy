Privacy Policy for P&P Team

Last updated: 20 October 2026

1. Who we are

P&P Team (the "app") is a work app for the staff of one company. It is published by Lenz B, United Arab Emirates ("we", "us"). Contact for privacy questions: mlabeeb707@gmail.com.

The app is invite-only. You cannot sign up yourself: your employer (an owner or manager) creates your account and gives you a one-time invite ID. If you do not work for the company, you have no account and we hold no data about you from this app, except as described in section 3 for customers' delivery details.

2. What the app is for

Staff check in and out of shifts by scanning a store QR code, delivery staff record delivery trips and the bills they deliver, and managers and owners see attendance, late returns, alerts and history for their stores.

3. Information we process

About employees and managers (entered by the employer or by you):

  • Full name, username, phone number, job type, store, role (owner, manager, staff, store account).
  • Your password. It is handled by our authentication provider and is never stored in readable form.
  • Attendance: check-in and check-out times, how the check-in was made (QR scan or a manual entry by the owner with a short written reason), extra-time entries and their approvals.
  • Delivery: trip start and back-at-store times, the bill numbers on the trip, the delivery place name and apartment or flat number chosen for each trip, whether a trip was late.
  • Alerts about late shifts or trips and the decisions taken on them.
  • A device identifier the app generates and sends when you activate your account and when a store phone signs in, and a "push token" that lets the notification service send alerts to your phone.
  • If you ask to delete your account: the request, its status, who decided it and when.
  • Last sign-in time, and an audit log of administrative actions (who changed what, when).

About customers: only what an employee types or scans for a delivery: a bill number, and a place name and apartment number. The app does not collect customer names, phone numbers, payment details or precise addresses.

We do not collect: your location (no GPS or location permission), contacts, photos or files from your phone, microphone audio, advertising identifiers, or analytics about your behaviour. The camera is used only to scan QR codes and bill numbers; images are processed on your phone and are not stored or sent to us.

4. Why we use it

To run the app: sign you in, record attendance and deliveries, show managers and owners their stores' history, send reminders and alerts, keep the service secure, and investigate problems. We do not use it for advertising or profiling, and we do not sell it.

5. Who receives it

  • Your employer (owners and managers of your store) see the information in section 3 that belongs to their stores. Owners see all stores; managers see their own store.
  • Service providers that run the app for us: Supabase (database, login and server functions, hosted in Seoul, South Korea); Expo (push notification service), Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging, Android) and Apple (APNs, iPhone) deliver notifications. They process data only to provide those services.
  • Messaging you start yourself: an owner or manager can send an invite message through WhatsApp or the phone's share sheet. That message goes through the app you choose, under its own policy.
  • We disclose information if the law requires it. We do not sell information and we do not share it for advertising.

6. Security

Data travels over HTTPS and access is limited by roles (owner, manager, staff, store account) enforced in the database. Please choose a strong password and keep it to yourself. No system is perfectly secure.

7. How long we keep it

  • Invite records are deleted automatically 90 days after they finish.
  • A phone's push token is removed when you sign out or when your account is turned off or deleted.
  • Attendance, delivery and alert records are the company's work records. They are kept for as long as the company uses the app, so owners can see history and handle disputes. When you delete your account they stay without your name (section 8).
  • When an account is turned off, you can no longer sign in and your sessions end.
  • Free-text reasons and notes that someone wrote, whether by you or about you (for example a note on a late delivery, the reason for a manual check-in, or an owner's note when deciding a request), and any copies of them in the audit records, stay as work records after an account is deleted. They are not cleaned, so please do not put personal details in notes.
  • Hosting logs and backups. Our hosting provider, Supabase, keeps platform logs (which can include sign-in details such as your username, IP address and the time) and database backups for its own retention period, and then they expire. We cannot delete them earlier. The period depends on our Supabase plan: platform logs are kept for about 1 day, and on our current plan no project backups are kept (if we upgrade, scheduled backups are kept for up to 7 days). Deleting your account does not remove you from those logs and backups before they expire.

8. Your choices and rights, including deleting your account

Delete your account inside the app: Account → Delete my account. You ask; your employer's owner approves (an owner's own request is approved by the app's administrator); nothing changes until it is approved, which is not instant. A request that is approved but not carried out expires after 7 days and you can ask again. After approval your login and sessions are deleted, your name is replaced by "Former employee", and your phone number, email, picture, username, device and push details are removed or replaced from the app's records. Your work records (shifts, deliveries, alerts) stay without your name, together with the free-text notes described in section 7. Logs and backups at our hosting provider expire on their own schedule (section 7). The request is refused while you are checked in or on a delivery (finish first), and the last owner cannot be deleted.

If your owner does not answer, email mlabeeb707@gmail.com with your full name, username and store; we ask the company to decide and tell you the result within 7 days. Details: Delete your account page.

You can also ask to see or correct your information through your employer or mlabeeb707@gmail.com. This policy and your use of the app are governed by the laws of the United Arab Emirates, and you may have further rights under those laws (for example access, correction and erasure).

9. Children

The app is for working adults and is not directed at children. You must be 18 or older to use it.

10. Changes

We will post changes here and change the date above. For significant changes the app or your employer will tell you.
